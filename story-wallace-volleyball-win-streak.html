Wallace State volleyball has won nine straight heading into Saturday

Published Saturday, October 17, 2026 by Cullman County Today

The Lions swept Bishop State and Lurleen B. Wallace on Friday and finish their home invitational Saturday with matches at 11 a.m. and 3 p.m. at Tom Drake Coliseum.

It has been a good fall to be a Wallace State volleyball fan. The Lions sit at 25 and 5 overall and 11 and 2 in conference play after two sweeps on Friday, the first day of the Wallace State Lions Invitational, based on the team's official results.

They beat Bishop State 25 to 9, 25 to 16 and 25 to 11 at noon. That evening they handled Lurleen B. Wallace 25 to 15, 25 to 16 and 25 to 17. That's nine wins in a row.

The tournament wraps up Saturday, Oct. 17, at Tom Drake Coliseum on campus in Hanceville. Wallace State plays Illinois Central College at 11 a.m. and Reid State at 3 p.m. Next week brings two more home dates: Calhoun on Wednesday, Oct. 21, at 5 p.m., and a doubleheader with Southern Union on Thursday, Oct. 22.
