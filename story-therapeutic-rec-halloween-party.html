Cullman Therapeutic Recreation invites everyone to a Halloween party on Oct. 24

Published Saturday, October 17, 2026 by Cullman County Today

Costumes, candy, a costume contest and hot dogs from Cullman police officers are on the menu at an accessible party for Therapeutic Recreation members. RSVPs are due Monday.

Cullman Therapeutic Recreation is throwing a Halloween party on Saturday, Oct. 24, at 5 p.m., and the theme is simple: everyone belongs here.

The party will be at Palomino RV Resort at Burrow Hamilton Farm, 1315 County Road 222 in Cullman. The resort is ADA accessible, so the evening is built to be comfortable for every guest.

Come in costume, because there will be candy and a costume contest. Officers from the Cullman Police Department will be grilling hot dogs, and Therapeutic Recreation members and resort guests will get a closed trick or treating route of their own so they can enjoy the tradition in a calm, safe setting.

Therapeutic Recreation is the city program that offers recreation and social time for people in our community with special needs and disabilities. The resort, the Cullman Police Department, Omni Sports Academy and Leldon's Locally Curated Goods are helping make the party happen.

Organizers ask that you RSVP by Monday, Oct. 19. The Cullman Tribune story linked below has the email address to use. The party is also on our Events page with directions and an add to calendar button.
