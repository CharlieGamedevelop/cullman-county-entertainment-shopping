Listerhill Credit Union opens its new Cullman branch and gives $10,000 to Cullman County Schools

Published Saturday, October 17, 2026 by Cullman County Today

The new branch on Industrial Drive SW is open for business, and the credit union marked the grand opening with a big check for the county school system.

Listerhill Credit Union celebrated the grand opening of its new Cullman branch on Thursday, Oct. 15, at 702 Industrial Drive SW. Crews broke ground on the building last November, and the staff moved in on Sept. 16.

The best news for local families came at the end of the ceremony. Listerhill handed Cullman County Schools a check for $10,000, and Superintendent Dr. Shane Barnette accepted it for the school system.

City and county leaders turned out for the event, including Mayor Woody Jacobs, Sheriff Matt Gentry, Sheriff elect Chad Whaley, Good Hope Mayor Jerry Bartlett and leaders from the Cullman Economic Development Agency and the Cullman Area Chamber of Commerce.

Credit union leaders noted that the ribbon cutting fell on International Credit Union Day. Listerhill got its start in 1952, when seven workers at the Reynolds Metals plant near Florence pooled $35 so their coworkers could borrow money at fair rates. Today it reports more than $1.5 billion in assets and about 92,000 members.

The Cullman branch is open Monday through Friday from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. and offers lending and mortgage help. You can reach it at (256) 383-9204, and you will find it in our Businesses directory too.
