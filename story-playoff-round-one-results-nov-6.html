All three Cullman County teams win their playoff openers and play again Friday

Published Monday, November 9, 2026 by Cullman County Today

Cold Springs, Good Hope and Vinemont each advanced on Friday night. All three second round games kick off at 7 p.m. this Friday, Nov. 13.

Cullman County went three for three in the first round of the AHSAA football playoffs on Friday night, Nov. 6.

Cold Springs beat Pisgah 42 to 34 in Class 2A in Bremen. Ethan Day ran 10 yards for the go ahead touchdown with 27 seconds left and then ran in the 2 point conversion. It was the first playoff win for the Eagles since 1993, and they improved to 10 and 1. Cold Springs now travels to Lexington (10 and 1) on Friday, Nov. 13.

Good Hope won 24 to 21 at Fairfield in double overtime in Class 4A. The Raiders led 14 to 0 in the fourth quarter before Fairfield tied it and forced overtime. Junior kicker Chase Prewett made a 34 yard field goal in the second overtime after an interception. Good Hope improved to 8 and 3 and hosts Deshler (9 and 2) on Friday.

Vinemont beat Central Florence 16 to 8 in Class 3A for the program's first postseason win since 1993 and finished the night at 7 and 4. The Eagles host Fayette County (9 and 2) on Friday, their first home playoff game in 33 years.

All second round kickoffs are at 7 p.m. For tickets and game details, check each school's own pages. Neighboring Addison is in Winston County, so that game stays off our Game Center.
