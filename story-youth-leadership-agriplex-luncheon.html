Youth Leadership students harvest and cook their own lunch at the Agriplex

Published Friday, October 16, 2026 by Cullman County Today

Students in the Cullman Area Chamber's Youth Leadership program picked vegetables and cooked a meal in the North Alabama Agriplex teaching kitchen.

On Thursday, Oct. 15, students in the Cullman Area Chamber of Commerce's Youth Leadership program got a hands on lesson in where food comes from at the North Alabama Agriplex, The Cullman Times reported.

They pulled sweet potatoes, cut kale and picked purple hull peas. Then they cooked lunch in the Agriplex's new teaching kitchen, with dishes like kale smoothies, roasted sweet potatoes and creamed peas. After eating with the Agriplex staff, they cleaned up and learned how composting works.
