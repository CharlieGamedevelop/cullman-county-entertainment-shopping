Oktoberfest drew a record 75,000 people, and Christkindlmarkt is next

Published Friday, October 16, 2026 by Cullman County Today

Organizers say the four day festival set records for attendance and hotel stays. Christkindlmarkt opens at Depot Park on Nov. 19 with free admission.

Cullman Oktoberfest had its biggest year yet. Cullman Parks, Recreation and Sports Tourism told The Cullman Tribune that about 75,000 people came out Oct. 1 through 4, and local hotels had record occupancy.

This was the first year the festival ran through Sunday. Even with a 70 percent chance of rain that day, nearly 10,000 people showed up. Organizers also made more room for craft vendors and say the two headline bands are already booked to come back next year.

Next up downtown is Cullman Christkindlmarkt at Depot Park, Nov. 19 through Dec. 23. Admission is free. Organizers are promising returning vendors, new food and one big new feature they are keeping secret for now.
