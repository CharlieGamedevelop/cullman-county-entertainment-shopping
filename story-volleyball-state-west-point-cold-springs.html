West Point reaches the 4A state semifinals and Cold Springs makes its first state trip since 1996

Published Friday, October 30, 2026 by Cullman County Today

West Point swept Satsuma before falling to top ranked Guntersville, and Cold Springs fell to Ariton in the Class 2A quarterfinals.

Two Cullman County volleyball teams played at the state tournament in Birmingham on Wednesday, Oct. 28, The Cullman Times reported.

West Point beat Satsuma in three sets, 25 to 17, 25 to 23 and 25 to 10, to reach the Class 4A semifinals. The Warriors then lost to top ranked Guntersville in three sets. West Point, ranked No. 7, finished the season 40 and 11.

Cold Springs lost to No. 5 Ariton in three sets in the Class 2A quarterfinals. It was the Eagles' first state tournament appearance since 1996, and they finished 18 and 21.

Congratulations to both teams on a great run.
