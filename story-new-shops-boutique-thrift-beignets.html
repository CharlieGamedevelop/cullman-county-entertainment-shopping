New in town: a downtown boutique, a thrift store and a beignet shop on the way

Published Wednesday, October 14, 2026 by Cullman County Today

A Little Too Much opened in the Warehouse District, America's Thrift Stores plans a November opening on 2nd Avenue SW, and Beignets and Brew is coming to Redstone Marketplace.

There is something new to shop downtown. A Little Too Much, a locally owned boutique, opened Oct. 1 at 104 1st Ave. NE in the Warehouse District, The Cullman Tribune reported. It carries colorful clothing, vintage finds and handmade items like pillows, purses and jewelry. The owner says new stock comes in every week and the Christmas windows go up Tuesday, Nov. 3.

America's Thrift Stores plans to open in November at 1721 2nd Ave. SW, Suite 1, where Bargain Hunt used to be. No exact date has been posted. The chain sells used clothing and household goods and is hiring for the Cullman store. Its Alabama charity partner is Make A Wish Alabama.

Beignets and Brew lists a Cullman shop as coming soon at 1269 2nd Ave. NW, Suite J, in Redstone Marketplace near 12th Way North. It will have a drive through. Expect New Orleans style beignets, coffee drinks and breakfast croissants. No opening date has been set.
