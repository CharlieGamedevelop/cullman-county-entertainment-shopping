Wallace State's new arts season opens Nov. 6 with Shakespeare set in the 1940s

Published Thursday, October 22, 2026 by Cullman County Today

Much Ado About Nothing kicks things off, followed by a free veterans concert, Ballet South's Nutcracker and a free Christmas Spectacular.

Wallace State Community College in Hanceville has posted its full Fine and Performing Arts season, and the fall half is packed.

Wallace State Theatre opens with Much Ado About Nothing on Nov. 6 and 7 at 7 p.m. and Nov. 8 at 2 p.m. This version moves Shakespeare's comedy to the World War II years, with the music and dances of that time. Admission is a $10 donation for adults and $5 for students and kids under 12.

The yearly Salute to United States Veterans follows on Nov. 10 at 7 p.m. The concert band and jazz band play patriotic music and the hymns of each branch of the military, and veterans in the audience are recognized. It is free.

Ballet South brings The Nutcracker to campus on Nov. 21 at 2 and 7 p.m. and Nov. 22 at 2 p.m. Tickets are $15 for adults and $10 for children. The free Christmas Spectacular with every Wallace State ensemble is Dec. 10 at 7 p.m.

All of these fall shows are in the Betty Leeth Haynes Theatre, and they are on our Events page with directions and Add to calendar buttons. The spring lineup includes Broadway Night on Feb. 25 and Thornton Wilder's Our Town in March.
