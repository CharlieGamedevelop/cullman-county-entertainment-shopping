About 500 seniors spent Senior Day at the Cullman County Fair

Published Saturday, October 24, 2026 by Cullman County Today

Folks 55 and older enjoyed a free lunch, live music and the fair exhibits on Oct. 21, and the crowd stood to honor local veterans.

Senior Day at the Cullman County Fair on Wednesday, Oct. 21, drew about 500 people ages 55 and older to the fairgrounds, The Cullman Tribune reported. Admission, parking and lunch were all free.

Cullman County Commission on Aging Director Stephanie Lawson said the weather was perfect and the fellowship made the day special. The Lions Club cooked extra corn dogs and hot dogs to feed the big group, and Jamie Looney and her Grand Ole Opry group provided the entertainment. Rides did not run, but guests walked the exhibits and picked up candied apples. Aveanna sponsored the event, and Premier Bank sponsored the lunch.

One of the best moments came when veterans were called up and given certificates while a patriotic song played, and everyone in the room stood to thank them. Several elected officials also spoke. Lawson noted that Sheriff Matt Gentry, Revenue Commissioner Barry Willingham and Commissioner Garry Marchman made their last Fair Day stage appearances in their current roles.

To learn about other activities for seniors, visit the Commission on Aging at co.cullman.al.us/coa or call 256-734-1241.
