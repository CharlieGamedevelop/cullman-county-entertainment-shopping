West Point and Cold Springs volleyball are headed to the state tournament

Published Friday, October 23, 2026 by Cullman County Today

Both teams won their opening matches at the North Super Regional in Hoover on Thursday, which locks up a spot in next week's AHSAA Elite Eight.

Two Cullman County volleyball teams are going to state. West Point and Cold Springs each won their first match at the AHSAA North Super Regional in Hoover on Thursday, Oct. 22, and every first round winner moves on to the state tournament.

West Point had the nail biter. The Lady Warriors took the first two sets against Sylacauga 25 to 19 and 25 to 13, then watched Sylacauga win the next two. West Point jumped out early in the fifth set and closed it out 15 to 8 to earn a Class 4A state berth.

Cold Springs had a smoother day in Class 2A and swept J.B. Pennington in three sets.

Vinemont and Holly Pond also made the trip to Hoover, but both saw their seasons end in the first round of the Class 3A bracket. Getting to the North Super Regional is a strong finish for both programs.

West Point was scheduled to face top ranked Guntersville in a North semifinal at 9 a.m. Friday, with more regional matches later in the day to settle seeding. The state tournament runs Tuesday through Thursday, Oct. 27 to 29, at the Birmingham CrossPlex and Bill Harris Arena.
