Country Wok in Hanceville adds ramen, wontons and matcha cannoli

Published Friday, October 23, 2026 by Cullman County Today

The Bangor Avenue Chinese restaurant that opened this year has rolled out three ramen bowls, Hong Kong style wontons, Japanese hot dogs and a matcha cannoli.

Country Wok, the Chinese restaurant on Bangor Avenue in Hanceville, has a handful of new dishes to try. Owner Kenny Lee opened the spot earlier this year, and he says customer feedback helps shape what goes on the menu.

The biggest addition is ramen, offered three ways. Shoyu ramen comes in a savory chicken broth, tori paitan ramen uses a rich and creamy chicken broth with black garlic oil, and miso ramen adds butter and sweet corn. All three come with chashu pork, a jammy egg, scallions and seasoned bamboo shoots.

There are also Hong Kong style pork and shrimp wontons served with the house Triple Street Sauce, plus Japanese hot dogs topped with Kewpie mayo, nori, bonito flakes and rice seasoning. For dessert, a matcha cannoli finishes things off.

Lee keeps the menu fairly small so the kitchen is not stretched thin, and he buys ingredients from local sources when he can.

Country Wok is at 107 Bangor Avenue, Suite A, and is open Tuesday through Saturday from 11 AM to 7 PM. You can order online at countrywok.com, through DoorDash, or by calling (256) 887-1360.
