County Commission thanks two outgoing commissioners and moves ahead on Stony Lonesome cabins

Published Thursday, October 22, 2026 by Cullman County Today

Commissioners Gary Marchman and Kerry Watson were honored for 12 years of service, and the board approved a new hazard plan, a lower speed limit on County Road 52 and new cabins at the off road park.

The Cullman County Commission took time at its Oct. 20 meeting to thank Commissioners Gary Marchman of District 2 and Kerry Watson of District 1, who are each finishing 12 years on the board. Chairman Jeff Clemons thanked them and their families for their service.

Richard Barnett will be sworn in for District 2 in November. Kenneth Walker, who chaired the commission from 2012 to 2020, will be sworn in for District 1.

The board adopted a new five year Hazard Mitigation Plan for the county and lowered the speed limit on County Road 52 to 25 miles per hour. It also proclaimed October as Domestic Violence Awareness Month.

Stony Lonesome OHV Park near Bremen is getting new places to stay. Using a federal Recreational Trails Program grant, the commission awarded the bids for accessible cabins, standard cabins and metal storage buildings to two building companies. A bid for security fencing was turned down because it did not meet the bid requirements.

Commissioners also approved a paving agreement for County Road 102 and an energy savings agreement for county buildings.
