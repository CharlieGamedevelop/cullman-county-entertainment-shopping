Seniors

Free Medicare help is by appointment only this year

Published Saturday, October 17, 2026 by Cullman County Today

Open Enrollment runs Oct. 15 through Dec. 7. The Cullman County Commission on Aging will review plans with you, but you need to call ahead.

Medicare Open Enrollment started Oct. 15 and runs through Dec. 7. This is the time to switch Medicare Advantage or drug plans, and any change you make starts Jan. 1, 2027.

The Cullman County Commission on Aging offers free counseling, but only by appointment, and it will not take walk ins this season. Call 256-734-1241 to get on the schedule. Spots are limited, The Cullman Tribune reported.

Bring your Medicare card, any other insurance cards and your actual medicine bottles. The office will not accept written lists or pharmacy printouts this year. If you want to be checked for Extra Help, bring proof of household income, including a current Social Security statement.

Rather do it by mail? Call and ask for a packet. It has to be back at the office by Friday, Nov. 13. The Commission on Aging is at 1519 Sportsman Lake Road NW. You can also call the State Health Insurance Assistance Program at 1-800-243-5463 or Medicare at 1-800-633-4227.
