Friday night football: Cold Springs wins its way to a home playoff game

Published Saturday, October 17, 2026 by Cullman County Today

Cold Springs and Vinemont won on Friday, Fairview rolled on Thursday, and five other county teams came up short. Here is how every Cullman County team did and where they play next.

Cold Springs had the biggest night in the county. The Eagles beat Southeastern 48 to 21 in Bremen behind four rushing touchdowns from Cooper Johnson. The Cullman Times reported that the win, along with other results in the region, locks up the No. 2 seed and the school's first home playoff game since 2003. Cold Springs is 7 and 1 and plays at Carbon Hill on Oct. 23.

Vinemont held on at Glencoe, 28 to 21. The Eagles led 22 to 0 at halftime, Glencoe scored twice late, and a fourth down sack sealed it. Vinemont is 5 and 4 and hosts Susan Moore on Oct. 23.

Fairview played a day early and scored six touchdowns in the first quarter of a 41 to 6 win over Crossville at Dafford Smith Stadium. The Aggies are 5 and 3, are off next week, and close the regular season at Cherokee County on Oct. 30.

Good Hope's six game winning streak ended with a reported 40 to 21 loss at Cherokee County. It was the Raiders' first region loss, and they are 6 and 2. Good Hope hosts Hayden on Oct. 23.

Cullman rallied with 17 points in the third quarter to get within 2 of Parker, ranked second in Class 5A, but Parker pulled away to win 42 to 24 at Woodard Stadium. Cullman plays at Jackson Olin on Oct. 23 at 7 p.m.

Close ones got away from two teams. Holly Pond led 16 to 8 at halftime but lost 17 to 16 at Susan Moore on a late field goal. West Point led in the fourth quarter at Lawrence County before losing 35 to 31. Holly Pond hosts Etowah on Oct. 23 and West Point hosts Deshler that same night.

Hanceville played Thursday because of fall break and lost to Hokes Bluff 49 to 8. The Bulldogs host Oneonta on Oct. 23. You can see every final and next game on our Game Center.
